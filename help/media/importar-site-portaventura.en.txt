To import a web page, open AINimation Studio and choose Sites from the home-page menu.

Open Options. Paste the page URL or select a saved site. We will use PortAventura NeXT.

Click Import page. AINimation creates an editable copy without changing the original website.

The preview shows the design and its components. Original scripts, canvas and live data need to be recreated.

Click text on the page to select its element. You can also use the components list.

In Advanced, change the text in its properties. The change applies only to your draft and can be undone.

Choose an animation and adjust its duration or delay. Effects respect the visitor’s reduced-motion preference.

Return to Options. Under Add image or video, choose Load from computer to include your files in the copy.

Expert is optional. Open its console and type slash help to see the commands available for your copy.

Download the project to keep editing, or export HTML. Local files are included. The original website stays unchanged.
